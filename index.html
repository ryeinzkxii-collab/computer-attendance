About / Problem

Attendance still runs on paper, in a school built for the digital age.

"Why does this matter?"

Manual attendance takes time, is prone to errors, and makes it difficult to monitor student presence. A computerized system can help address these challenges and improve attendance management for Grade 12 students at Britech College.
✕

Manual Attendance

  • Takes up class time to call names or pass a sheet
  • Prone to human error and mismarked entries
  • Makes it difficult to monitor student presence in real time
  • Records are harder to search, total, and report on
VS
✓

Computerized System

  • Logs a student's presence in seconds at check-in
  • Reduces mismarked or missed entries
  • Provides staff with a current view of attendance records
  • Turns attendance records into data that is easier to review

Manual attendance-taking takes time, is prone to errors, and makes it difficult to monitor student presence. This study looks at whether a computerized system helps address these problems and how it affects accuracy and efficiency for Grade 12 Alcaraz at Britech College.
